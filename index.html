The Kohlmann Family

Two families, one from each parent

This site follows the paternal line and the maternal line back from the people living now to the records behind them: where they lived, what they did, and how the two sides meet.

How this is put together

It started with family testimony and a one-page chart of the Eifler and Becker line, compiled on 18 January 2007. The chart named people and dates. The records came after: a 1926 marriage entry in Dresden, birth and death entries, Bautzen directories, a Handelsregister notice, and the firm's own letterhead and 1943 invoice.

Where a record and the chart disagree, the record is what the site uses. Richard Becker's chart has his birth and death days the other way round from his 1938 death entry, so the entry wins. Where no record has been found, the page says so. Alvin Kohlmann's birth and death are still unknown. Rita's father's first name is still unknown. Sigrid's death is not recorded.

The map draws a place only for someone who has died. Towns are wide circles. A street from a directory or a register is a small circle around today's house number, because numbers can change. Living people stay off the map.

The two families

The paternal line

Kohlmann, Eifler, Becker and Just

The oldest Becker on the chart is Richard Hermann Becker, a merchant, born 5 January 1875 in Herzberg (Elster) and died at his shop and home, Rothermundtstraße 5 in Dresden, on 9 January 1938. He was cremated at the crematorium in Tolkewitz. His wife Clara Elise, née Just, was born 1 December 1880 in Raun, in the Vogtland, and died 27 December 1954 at Wilthener Straße 19 in Bautzen.

Their daughter Gertrud Elise was born 22 January 1905 at Ziegelstraße 2 in Dresden. On 2 October 1926 she married Ernst Alwin Eifler, born 7 October 1892 in Sohland an der Spree. By 1928 he was sole owner of Weickert & Eifler in Bautzen, a saddlery in 1921 that was a steel-wire mattress factory at Gnaschwitz by 1928. He died on 18 December 1968. The chart says Bautzen. A death entry for that day has not been found, so the place stays family tradition. Gertrud died at the same Bautzen house on 11 December 1973.

Their younger daughter Christa, born 25 November 1928 in Bautzen, married Alvin Kohlmann there on 24 July 1952 and died in Starnberg on 21 May 2006. Her older sister Sigrid was born in Bautzen on 20 July 1927 and married a Michaelis there on 20 August 1953. No death is recorded for Sigrid, and Alvin's own birth, death and occupation are not yet known. Their son Thomas was born in Radebeul in 1956. He has a brother, Matthias, and a sister, Sabine.

The maternal line

Werner and Woltmann

Rita was born in Döbeln in 1956. Her birth surname was Woltmann, her mother Brigitte Woltmann's name, because Brigitte and Rita's father were not married then. The surname was later changed to Werner, her father's surname. His first name is not known, and it is not known whether he and Brigitte ever married.

Rita has two sisters, Heide and Annette. Their maiden surnames are not confirmed, and no town is recorded for Brigitte, for Rita's father, or for the sisters. What is known about this line is still mostly family testimony.

m. 1980

Thomas left East Germany first and later helped Rita leave. They married in Munich in 1980. The route and the year of each crossing are not yet recorded. Their daughters are Valeria, born 27 June 1990 in Munich, and Elisa, born 25 March 1994 in Munich. Valeria married Sebastian Belfiore in Munich in September 2022. Their son Enzo was born in Tampa, Florida, in January 2026, and holds US, UK and German citizenship.
