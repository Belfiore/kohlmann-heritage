The Name

“Kohlmann” is an occupational German surname meaning coal man or charcoal burner, from Kohle (coal) and Mann (man). It originally identified someone who produced charcoal in the forests — a skilled and physically demanding trade that supplied fuel to blacksmiths, foundries, and households across pre-industrial Europe. The surname was historically common across German-speaking regions. This particular Kohlmann line is currently traceable to Saxony, where Thomas Kohlmann was born in Radebeul in 1956. Thomas Kohlmann & Rita Kohlmann (née Werner)

Rita has two sisters, Heide and Annette. Thomas escaped from East Germany to West Germany; he later helped Rita get out. They married in 1980 in Munich.
