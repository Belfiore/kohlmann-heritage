The Family Connection

The “Eifler” in Weickert & Eifler is, with high probability, Ernst Alwin Eifler (1892–1968), Thomas Kohlmann's maternal grandfather. He is documented as a Fabrikant — factory owner — on the family Ahnentafel compiled in 2007. He was born 7 October 1892 in Sohland an der Spree, married Gertrud Elise Becker on 2 October 1926 in Dresden, and lived and worked in Bautzen until his death on 18 December 1968 in a fatal car accident. His daughter, Elise Christa Kohlmann (née Eifler), was Thomas's mother.

This identification is treated here as a strong working hypothesis rather than a confirmed fact. To anchor it in primary documentation, three lines of evidence are being pursued:

  • Handelsregister (commercial register) entries for Weickert & Eifler held in the Sächsisches Staatsarchiv, which would name the firm's principals.
  • Surviving company materials — letterheads, invoices, advertisements — that would tie Ernst Alwin Eifler personally to the firm.
  • Family testimony from Thomas Kohlmann and his siblings (Matthias, Sabine).

Once any of the above is in hand, this section will be revised to state the connection as fact rather than hypothesis. Until then, nothing about the company itself or about Ernst Alwin's specific role in it is being invented here.
